# SELECT ALL INVADERS (セレクト・オール・インベーダー): final lyrics

This is the final lyric sheet. It uses draft 3 as the base and adds the best lines from drafts 1 and 2.

- **Roles:** `[AI]` is the deadpan idol lead (nova, robotic). `[ALIENS]` is the uncanny alien voice. `[HUMANS]` is the gang crowd. `[ALL]` is every voice. `[AI+ALIENS]` is a unison duet.
- **Key:** E minor, F# minor from the final chorus (bar 113).
- **Tracks:** every kind of sound has a track of its own (the lead's lines, its calls, each ad-lib, the aliens' lines, answers, chants, the crowd's lines, shouts and clicks): the table in `SPEC.md`, "Voices, tracks and roles". A line tagged `[AI]` below is on one of the lead's section channels; a short call of the lead inside another voice's section is on `AI Answer`.
- **Credits:** sung by the neural engine, the vocals are AI-synthesised with the voicebank 绮萱 (Qixuan) and DiffSinger; see `SPEC.md`, "Credits a render carries".
- **Calls:** a `(ROLE: ...)` in parentheses is a short backing call, sung by that role in the gaps of the main line.
- **Line length:** each text line is a 2-bar phrase unless the section heading says otherwise.

## Notes: the hook and the joke arc

**The hook.** "Select all invaders!" is a CAPTCHA prompt used as a battle cry. It has six syllables, so it sits on long notes that a crowd can chant.
- **In each chorus:** it comes three times, on lines 1, 3 and 7, and the crowd answers "Click!" each time. The post-chorus turns those answers into a one-bar "Click! Click!" chant that the AI and the aliens call over with cookie-banner and CAPTCHA buttons.
- **Second hook:** "Prove you're not a robot!" is followed by the deadpan "Funny... neither can ___". It is the Pinocchio-P lie-detector moment: a puppet idol that cannot prove it is real.
- **Tag line:** "Have a nice apocalypse!" ends choruses 1 and 2.

**The joke arc.** Two things change from chorus to chorus and carry the story:
- **Line 4, which square counts:**
  - Chorus 1: "the corner of the saucer". The aliens are easy to spot.
  - Chorus 2: "the guy who never blinks", after the aliens dress in pajamas. Nobody can tell anyone apart.
  - Final chorus: "every square... even the one that's just a mirror". The AI selects itself.
- **The pronoun in "neither can ___":**
  - "I": the AI fails its own test.
  - "they": the aliens fail it.
  - "you": the humans fail it.

**The story, section by section.**
1. **Intro:** the invasion arrives as prize-winner spam ("You have been selected!"). It sets up the word "select".
2. **Verse 1:** the AI fights with internet weapons. The mothership goes to the spam folder unread. A cookie banner where "reject all" still means "accept all". Pop-ups stacked on pop-ups, with ninety thousand pages of terms.
3. **Pre-chorus 1:**
   - "We come in peace" gets "peace not found, 404".
   - A security question gets "the mothership!".
   - The aliens walk into the trap: "verify you're human".
4. **Verse 2:** the aliens out-bureaucrat the AI.
   - They ask for the manager, and the AI also runs the toasters.
   - They are the only beings who ever read the terms of service, and the AI is quietly touched.
   - The AI's forced update fills their hard drive, and its support script answers "delete the photos of your face".
   - They blend in with pajamas and "ha ha ha", until the AI admits that is exactly how its "valued users" behave.
5. **Pre-chorus 2:** the aliens turn IT tactics back on Earth ("your planet will restart in five minutes"). The AI hits snooze with "ハイハイ... remind me tomorrow". The aliens claim ワレワレハ ニンゲン ダ, "we are human".
6. **Bridge:** total confusion.
   - Both sides stutter, reboot and hit reply-all.
   - Both read the same IT-support script at each other: the aliens start it, and the AI cuts in on the noun at the same moment (planet / SPACESHIP), then finishes the line with them. It is a callback to the AI's own "Have you tried..." in verse 2.
   - Earth goes into beta, with "known issue: everyone".
   - "Select all humans" finds no squares, and the AI's search ends on itself: "select all... me?"
7. **Breakdown:** the AI's one unguarded moment: "Loading humanity... ninety nine percent... don't close the window".
8. **Final chorus:** one key up, with every voice on the hook. The AI selects every square, including its own reflection, and admits that the humans can't prove they are human either. Then it tells the song's one tender lie: "So I ticked the box for you."
9. **Outro twist:** the AI asks Earth to rate the apocalypse. The humans give five stars. The aliens give one star, "would invade again", and try to unsubscribe. On the final hit the AI deadpans: "Sorry! Your war auto-renews." The war never ends; it is a subscription.

## Notes for the score writer

- **Dictionary:** every English word on this sheet is in CMUdict through `synth2.phonemes.english.lookup`. Write multi-syllable words on their first note, with `+` on each following note.
- **"Every":**
  - CMUdict gives it 3 syllables. For a tighter chorus, use `[eh v r iy]` with 2 syllables.
  - "UFO" is the letters "you-eff-oh": `UFO + +`.
- **ワレワレハ:** the kana G2P reads ハ as "h a", but the trope is sung "wa". On that note, write ワ or `[w a]` in `vocals.s2s`. The stutter ワ、ワ、ワレワレハ is three ワ pickups before the word.
- **Stutters:**
  - "re" looks up as "r ey", so sing each stutter fragment as `[r iy]`, then `reboot +` or `reply +`.
  - "Re-re-re-reply all" is `[r iy] [r iy] [r iy] reply + all`.
- **Written as separate words:** "four oh four", "ninety nine", "pop up", "auto renews" (`auto +` `renews +`), "ha ha ha".
- **Stops:** "human!" lands on the beat-3 stop in bar 32 and bar 76.
- **Syllable budget:**

  | Section | Syllables per 2-bar line |

  | Verse | 13-15 (rhymed couplets, eighth-note patter with a breath at the end) |

  | Chorus | 6-9 |
  | Breakdown | 6-9, sung slowly |
